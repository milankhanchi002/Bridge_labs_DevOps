                      AZURE CLOUD ADMINISTRATION NOTES
               MODULE 6: AZURE ACCOUNT, SUBSCRIPTION & BILLING

1. HIERARCHY SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Management Group  -> Central Governance (Policies, RBAC across multiple subscriptions)
Subscription      -> Billing Boundary & Quota Container (Generates individual invoice)
Resource Group    -> Lifecycle & Deployment Boundary (Cascading deletion)
Resource          -> Actual Cloud Service (VM, Storage, Database, VNet)


2. SUBSCRIPTION TYPES AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Free Trial      : $200 USD (₹15,800 INR) credit for 30 days + 12 mo popular free + Always free.
- Pay-As-You-Go   : Consumption-based monthly billing (No upfront commitment).
- Student         : $100 USD credit/year, no credit card required.
- Enterprise (EA) : Multi-year enterprise financial commitment with enterprise discounts.
- CSP             : Managed & billed via certified Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider partner.


3. ESSENTIAL COST OPTIMIZATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. VMs must be in `Stopped (Deallocated)` state to stop compute billing.
2. Delete orphaned Managed Disks and Public IPs after destroying VMs.
3. Configure Budgets & Alerts at 50%, 80%, and 100% thresholds.
4. Apply Resource Group lifecycle boundaries to tear down dev/test setups cleanly.
5. Use Azure Advisor Cost recommendations for right-sizing resources.
